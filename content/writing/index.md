# Academic Writing

Style and reporting conventions for quantitative psychology manuscripts.

## Style

- APA 7 as the default frame for psychology venues, and where specific
  journals deviate.
- Sentence-level conventions this lab holds beyond the manuals: claims
  attributed, hedging only where uncertainty is real, one idea per sentence.

## Reporting standards

- APA JARS-Quant for study reporting.
- What a methods section must contain for the study to be replicable from the
  manuscript alone.
- Reporting model results: estimates with uncertainty, fit, and the
  comparison set.

## Mathematics in manuscripts

- Notation consistent within a paper; [Notation](/notation) is the default
  starting point.
- Every symbol defined at first use.
- Likelihoods and generative models set out in full when they are the
  contribution.
