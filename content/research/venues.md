# Journals and Venues

*Last verified: 2026-10-13. This page is volatile; re-verify before relying on
it.*

Where quantitative psychology and psychometrics work lands, and how the
venues differ in ways that matter when choosing one.

## What varies

- Review model: single or double blind, number of rounds typical.
- Speed: median days from submission to first decision.
- Scope: methods only, or methods plus application.
- Archival weight: citation half-life versus conference proceedings.
- Open data and code policy, and whether it is enforced.

## Notes

- Psychometrika, British Journal of Mathematical and Statistical Psychology,
  Applied Psychological Measurement, Psychological Methods, and the
  educational-measurement journals differ more in scope than in prestige
  ordering, which shifts slowly.
- Conference proceedings in this field are rarely archival; treat them as
  preview surfaces.
