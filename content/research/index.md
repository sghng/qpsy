# Research

How the research itself runs: methodology, the pipeline from question to
report, where work is published, and how review works.

## Methodology and pipeline

The pipeline from research question to report, in the order the work actually
proceeds:

1. Question and falsifiable prediction.
2. Design: measurement model, conditions, sampling.
3. Power and precision, decided before data collection.
4. Preregistration where the design allows it.
5. Data collection and integrity checks.
6. Analysis per the modeling workflow in
   [Statistical Modeling](/modeling/).
7. Reporting per [Academic Writing](/writing/).
