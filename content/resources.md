# Resources

This page lists the principal textbooks and standalone references used in
developing qpsy. Entries provide bibliographic details and external retrieval
links only; qpsy does not host or reproduce their full text. Access to a linked
text may require purchase or institutional access.

Paper references appear on individual notes and in the [Registry](/registry/),
with DOI links as the canonical retrieval paths where available. A paper does
not need a registry entry merely because it is cited.

## Source tags

Source pointers pair a direct link with an Obsidian nested tag, such as
`#ref/mdk`. Search for a tag in Obsidian to find every associated note; on the
published site, the tag opens a page listing those notes. The same tag appears
in the canonical resource entry below. The tag is a stable source identifier,
not a substitute for the full citation.

## Textbooks

### Statistical Inference

George Casella and Roger L. Berger. *Statistical Inference*. 2nd ed. CRC Press,
2024. This is a reprint of the second edition originally published in 2001.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781003456285) ·
[Publisher](https://www.routledge.com/Statistical-Inference/Casella-Berger/p/book/9781032593036)
#ref/casella-berger

Canonical reference for [Parameter Estimation](/modeling/estimation),
[Maximum Likelihood Estimation](/modeling/maximum-likelihood), and
[the EM Algorithm](/modeling/em-algorithm).

### Designing Experiments and Analyzing Data

Scott E. Maxwell, Harold D. Delaney, and Ken Kelley. *Designing Experiments and
Analyzing Data: A Model Comparison Perspective*. 3rd ed. Routledge, 2018.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.4324/9781315642956) ·
[Companion site](https://designingexperiments.com/) #ref/mdk

Canonical reference for the [Analysis of Variance](/topics/anova/) collection,
including model comparison, contrasts, multiple comparisons, and power.
