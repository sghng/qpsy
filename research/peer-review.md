# Peer Review

*Last verified: 2026-10-13.*

The review process from both sides: what a reviewer owes a manuscript, and
what an author owes the review.

## The process

- What happens between submission and first decision.
- What associate editors actually do with a review.
- Revision rounds as negotiation, not verdict.

## Reviewing well

- Read for the claim the authors are making, not the paper you would have
  written.
- Separate fatal flaws from preferences, and label which is which.
- Verify before objecting: a checkable objection is worth ten impressions.
- Timeliness is part of the obligation.

## As an author

- Respond point by point; never argue tone in a response letter.
- Pre-empt the obvious objections in the manuscript instead of the rebuttal.

## Registry

Reviews completed for research are logged in the [Registry](/registry/),
one entry per paper, descriptive only.
